🧑‍🏫 Facilitator Tips

💬 Be Conversational

Write prompts like you're talking to a colleague. Natural language works best.

🔄 Build Incrementally

Add one feature at a time. Ask follow-ups to refine and improve.

🎨 Describe Visuals

Use plain English: "rounded corners," "soft blue," "center it" — Copilot understands.

❓ Ask Why

Say "explain this code" to learn alongside building. Understanding matters.

✨ Iterate, Don't Restart

If something's not right, say "that's close, but can you also..." and refine.

🚀 Embrace Momentum

Perfect code isn't the goal—productive conversation with AI is. Keep iterating!
