🎤 Facilitator Tip — Embrace the Vibe

Encourage participants to speak naturally to Circuit and GitHub Copilot — there's no "magic syntax." The best prompts sound like instructions you'd give a junior developer:

✅ Do: "Add a button that clears all completed tasks when clicked."
❌ Avoid: "function clearCompleted() { ... }"
  • 🗣️ Describe the outcome, not the implementation — let the AI choose the "how."
  • 🔁 Iterate in small steps — one feature per prompt works better than a giant request.
  • 🧭 If the output isn't right, rephrase — add context like "make it simpler" or "use CSS Grid instead."
  • 🎉 Celebrate the vibe — the goal is flow and confidence, not perfection.

Remind everyone: non-technical participants often write the best prompts because they describe what they want in human terms. That's the magic of vibe coding! ✨
