## 💬 How to Use in the Workshop

1. Open the **Live App** link above on your device
2. Navigate through labs using the top tab bar
3. Each lab shows a working demo on the left and a guide panel on the right
4. The guide panel includes **ready-to-use Copilot prompts** — click **Copy** and paste into Circuit or GitHub Copilot
5. Participants recreate the lab from scratch using only the prompts — no peeking at the source! 😄

---

## 💡 Facilitator Tip

> Encourage participants to **speak naturally** — the best prompts sound like instructions you'd give a junior developer.
> 
> ✅ **Do:** *"Add a button that clears all completed tasks when clicked."*  
> ❌ **Avoid:** *"function clearCompleted() { ... }"*

Non-technical participants often write the best prompts because they describe what they want in human terms. **That's the magic of vibe coding!** ✨
